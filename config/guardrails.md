# Content Guardrails — what NEVER to write

These rules apply to every piece of content the agents produce. Hard rules. No exceptions.

## Voice / authenticity

- **Never invent personal trading experiences.** No "I was wrecked today by Apex's trailing dd" unless it actually happened. Fabricated trader stories are the fastest way to destroy brand trust in the futures community.
- **Never use fake "Anyone else…" hooks** designed to look organic. Astroturf is a brand-killer.
- **No buzzwords:** game-changer, leverage your edge, take it to the next level, trading edge unlocked, level up, crush it, dominate, secret weapon, holy grail.
- **No emoji spam.** The Shadow Edge brand uses 🎯 sparingly and that's it.
- **No fake urgency.** "Only 24 hours left" / "limited spots" / countdown timers (unless we genuinely have a deadline like founders'-pricing-ends-July-31).

## Compliance / legal

- **Never make profit claims.** No "doubled my account" / "guaranteed returns" / "this tool will make you money."
- **Never imply outcomes are guaranteed.** Always lean on "discipline aid", "risk management tool", "compliance dashboard."
- **Always honor "what it is NOT" disclaimers** from the product copy: not a signal generator, not a guarantee, not a substitute for the broker's own risk system.
- **Never reproduce prop firm rules verbatim** — paraphrase + link to their official rule page. Their rules change; we don't want to be the source of stale rule claims.
- **Never use prop firm logos in promotional images** without checking trademark/usage policies.
- **Never use NinjaTrader trademarks deceptively.** "NinjaTrader 8 add-on" is fine. "Official NinjaTrader product" is not.

## Posting hygiene

- **No spam in subreddits.** If posting to r/FuturesTrading, r/Daytrading, r/propfirm: value-first, no link in post body. Mention the tool only if directly asked in comments. Otherwise let the username and post history do the work.
- **No DMing strangers cold with sales pitches.** DMs are reserved for influencer outreach with a specific reason, referencing their actual content.
- **No copy-paste comments across multiple subs.** Reddit's spam filters catch this and ban accounts.

## When in doubt
The Director (Jordan) has final review. If a piece feels even slightly off, kill it. Cost of bad content is high; cost of skipping a piece is zero.
